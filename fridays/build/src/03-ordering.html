Concept, web and kiosk

Order the way
you order at the window

Today an order goes through a generic embedded widget that was never told what a trùk 'i pan is. It flattens a protein and format grid into a long list, quotes the same wait at eight in the evening and at midnight, and has no idea how deep the queue is. This is the version that knows.

What breaks now

Four failures,
one root cause

The ordering layer does not model the kitchen. Everything else follows from that.

{{ICON:list}} A grid sold as a list

Eleven cuts against seven formats is seventy-seven cells, eighteen of them things the kitchen does not do, so fifty-nine real ways to order a plate in a shape a customer reads in one glance. Poured into a generic menu it becomes dozens of near-identical rows nobody scrolls to the end of.

{{ICON:timer}} A wait nobody quotes

Reviews put the walk-up wait at around forty minutes on a busy night. The order screen says nothing, so the surprise lands at the window where it costs the most.

{{ICON:chef-hat}} Tickets that arrive out of order

Four chicken plates spread across four tickets get grilled four times instead of once. The kitchen batches by hand, from memory, at one in the morning.

{{ICON:money}} Prices in a retired currency

The printed menu still quotes ƒ and Ang. The Netherlands Antillean guilder stopped being legal tender on 1 July 2025. The island now runs on the Caribbean guilder.

The other half

The rail that
makes the quote true

A promised time is a lie unless the kitchen can keep it. Tickets land on a rail grouped by cut, so the grill cook fires four chicken once instead of four times, and the system knows how deep the queue really is before it quotes the next customer.

{{ICON:sparkle}}
Reading the rail The batch hint is computed from the tickets below, and it changes as they land.

The governor

What happens
when it gets bad

Every ordering system on earth quotes twenty minutes and hopes. This one watches the rail and changes its own behaviour three times on the way up.

1 to 6 tickets

Quote it and take it

Fire time is quoted straight, orders flow, nothing is throttled.

7 to 12 tickets

Quote honestly, open the early lane

The quote climbs with the rail, and Trempan slots for the next night surface in the flow so the peak starts flattening itself.

13 up tickets

Stop promising

New tickets get a range rather than a number, delivery pauses, and the truck says so instead of quietly taking money for a forty minute wait.

Band widths are the share of a Friday or Saturday session, 21:00 to 04:00, that the modelled queue spends in each state: roughly 41, 41 and 17 per cent.
