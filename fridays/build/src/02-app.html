Fridays currently rides on a white-label of a shared island ordering platform. It works, but the brand is a skin on someone else's checkout, and the app answers the wrong question. Nobody opens a food app at half past eleven to browse. They open it to find out how long, and when to leave the house.

The shape of it

Three decisions
the rest follows from

{{ICON:ticket}}

The ticket is the app

Not a menu, not a feed. Once you have ordered, the app is a single live ticket with a fire timeline and a collection code. Everything else is how you get to one.

Why it mattersCharcoal is not a microwave. If the wait is real, the honest move is to show it minute by minute rather than spin a progress bar.

{{ICON:car}}

It tells you when to leave

The app knows the queue depth and how far away you are. So it stops reporting status and starts giving an instruction: leave in six minutes and your food hits the window as you park.

Why it mattersFood that waits for a customer goes soft. A customer who waits at the window blocks the next twelve. Timing the handover fixes both.

{{ICON:sun-horizon}}

It pays you to come early

Trempan is a lane, not a discount code. Book a collection slot in the first two hours after the grill lights, 21:00 to 23:00, and the kitchen guarantees twelve minutes and takes Cg 2 off, because an empty grill is worth more to them than a full one.

Why it mattersThe truck's only real problem is that everyone arrives between 22:30 and 01:00. Loyalty that moves demand beats loyalty that stamps a card.

The screens

Live, not mocked

Every phone below is a working interface. The tab bars switch, the builder builds, and the fire times are computed against real Curacao time, so these screens read differently at nine in the evening and at two in the morning. Tap around.

What changes

ON THE SHARED PLATFORM

  • {{ICON:x}}The menu arrives as a flat list, so the protein and format grid the kitchen actually runs on is flattened into hundreds of near-duplicate lines.
  • {{ICON:x}}No queue depth, so the quoted time is the same at 20:00 and at midnight.
  • {{ICON:x}}The customer belongs to the platform. Fridays cannot message a regular or see who they are.
  • {{ICON:x}}English and Dutch only, on an island whose first language is Papiamentu.
  • {{ICON:x}}Commission on every order, on a Cg 21 plate.

ON THEIR OWN APP

  • {{ICON:check}}The builder mirrors the window: pick the cut, pick the format, batata or pan, then sauce.
  • {{ICON:check}}Fire time is quoted from the live rail, so a busy Saturday quotes the high thirties and a quiet Tuesday quotes about ten.
  • {{ICON:check}}Fridays keeps the relationship, the order history and the reason someone stopped coming.
  • {{ICON:check}}Papiamentu first, with Dutch, English and Spanish behind it.
  • {{ICON:check}}Card fees only, and a cash-at-the-window option that costs nothing.
