A research-led redesign of fridays.cw for Curacao's best known trùk 'i pan. A marketing site, a native app and an ordering system, each built on the same idea: a food truck has exactly one interface, a lit window in the side of a van at night, and every digital surface should behave like it.

Eleven cuts against seven formats is seventy-seven cells, eighteen of which are things the kitchen does not do, leaving fifty-nine real combinations and sixty-eight printed prices. Every surface Fridays owns flattens that shape into a list or a photograph of a poster.

The wait reviewers report on a busy night. No site, app or checkout anywhere quotes it, so the surprise lands at the hatch.

1page, one language

fridays.cw is a single English page with the menu as a 1.5 MB scan. Curacao's first language is Papiamentu.

The menu still prices in Antillean guilders, which stopped being legal tender on 1 July 2025. The island runs on the Caribbean guilder now.

Honest notes

What is real and
what is modelled

Real

Every plate price, format, opening hour, address, phone number and review is transcribed from Fridays' own December 2024 printed menu and from fridays.cw. The photography is theirs. The Caribbean guilder changeover, the trùk 'i pan history and the Papiamentu vocabulary are sourced and cited in the README.

Modelled

Queue depth and fire time are simulated from a curve peaking between 22:30 and 01:00. They move with real Curacao time, so the pages read differently at nine in the evening and at two in the morning, but no live kitchen is feeding them. Trempan, the fire ticket, the kitchen rail and the governor are proposals, not features that exist today. The prices for things the printed menu does not carry are proposed too. That is the whole list of them: rice, funchi or salad in place of fries at Cg 2, the mitar mitar second grill pass at Cg 3, extra batata at Cg 4, delivery at Cg 8, and the Cg 2 Trempan credit. Every other price on these pages is transcribed.

Needs a native pass

The Papiamentu was written from sourced vocabulary, corrected once in review, and still not checked by a native speaker. Before anything ships, a Curacao copywriter should go through it line by line.

Worth asking the owner

The banner on the truck itself reads "closed on Thuesday", while fridays.cw lists all seven days open. The hours here follow the website. The printed menu also shows two prices for chicken at Regular, read here as a small and a large, and files Surf and Turf Deluxe inside the Premium table rather than as its own line.
