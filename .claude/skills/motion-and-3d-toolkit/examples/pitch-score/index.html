What a phone-to-3D swing score could look like for baseball: film a bullpen, rebuild the body in 3D, read one card per phase of the delivery, and get a score. The skeleton is the scripted Pitch Lab pitcher. The ranges, the weights and the uncertainty are illustrative and unvalidated. This is a concept and is not affiliated with any company.

What it shows, and what it does not

  • Every number is computed from the synthetic skeleton, so the demo is consistent with itself and says nothing about a real pitcher.
  • The target ranges are placeholders. The hip-shoulder range (35 to 65°) is the one Motion Studio uses, from the sources it cites; the others are rough and unvalidated.
  • Each value carries a range (±5° or ±4 points) to show how an estimate can be presented honestly. That size is not measured for any phone pipeline.
  • The score is an unweighted mean of the five card scores. No score like this has been validated against outcomes.
  • Stride length as a share of height needs the athlete's real height; 1.80 m is assumed here.
