How the score works

  • The cards are the nine attractors of the programme, with the names the ModernPitching app uses and what good looks like in the coaches' words.
  • The programme does not measure attractors; coaches judge them by eye. Each card therefore uses a proxy that 3D joints can give (an angle, a path, an order or a timing), with a start range that is this demo's own guess. Calibrate those ranges with the coaches before anyone relies on them.
  • Inside the start range scores 100, close (within a quarter of its width) is amber, further out red. A proxy is only scored when the frame rate can resolve it, and an attractor without a proxy (sagittal: waist folding needs the pelvis's own tilt) is shown as not measured, never guessed. No total with fewer than five scored attractors.
  • Body parts drawn see-through were not seen by the cameras in that frame; their position is filled in for display.
