How the score works

  • The structure follows a phone-to-3D swing app: a card per phase with a value, green when inside the range, amber when close (within a quarter of the range width), red when further out, one cue, and a 0–100 score with a bar per phase.
  • The ranges are the ones Motion Studio uses for pitching, with the studies named on each card. A value inside the range scores 100; outside, the score drops in proportion to the distance.
  • A phase is only scored when the frame rate can resolve it (stride and rotation from 25 fps, the arm at foot contact from 45 fps, release from 100 fps). Phases that are not scored are left out of the total, never filled in.
  • Body parts drawn see-through were not seen by the cameras in that frame; their position is filled in for display.
