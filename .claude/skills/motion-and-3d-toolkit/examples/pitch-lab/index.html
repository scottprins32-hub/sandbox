Can the estimate be trusted?

estimated, synthetic data

Claim tested: peak hip-shoulder separation from video is within ±5° of the lab system, on 40 simulated pitches. The bound was fixed before the data.

Equivalent on average, not on every pitch. The mean difference sits well inside ±5°, but one pitch can be off by 7°. Both statements are true, and a coach needs the second one.

The estimate lags the reference by samples. At 60 fps that is ms, a few frames of release timing.

150 frames at 1280×720, rendered without a graphics card in about 14 seconds. Frame i is drawn from i alone: five frames rendered twice, in a different order, came out byte-identical.

How this was made

  • Statistics: estimated-motion-validation. The script reproduces a published ICC table before it is trusted.
  • Video: deterministic-frame-rendering. This page renders frame i from i alone, so the same video comes out every time.
  • Page: web-design-review-loop. Screenshots at three widths, a machine audit, a squint test, fixes.
  • The figure is hand-authored: ten phase poses blended with cubic curves and two-bone leg IK. It is an illustration of how such a viewer behaves, not pitching biomechanics.
