Data sheet

Five primes, one drawing.

Every element in the set is ground from the same three glass melts, so the colour of the 24 is the colour of the 135. The coating is laid down in one run per set, eleven layers, in a room that never sees daylight.

Focus throws are 300 degrees on every barrel, the front diameter is 114 mm across the set, and the gear positions line up so a follow-focus unit moves between lenses without being reset.
